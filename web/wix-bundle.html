GRANT RESOURCES

What is covered — and how to use it

This is more than a directory. Each resource is classified by how much of the call can currently be verified and normalised safely.

UK research funding

UKRI, NIHR and Wellcome are represented with structured-beta coverage where source pages expose stable fields. Use these for research, health and care, innovation and translational funding.

Show UK sources →

European funding

EU Funding & Tenders covers Horizon Europe and related calls. The engine prefers the European Commission’s structured route rather than brittle page scraping.

Show European sources →

Africa & Global Majority

Science for Africa, IDRC, WHO/TDR, Grand Challenges Canada, Global Innovation Fund and related sources are surfaced with explicit access and source-state labels.

Show Global Majority sources →

Global health & humanitarian

CEPI, Gavi, Elrha, Fogarty and NIH are tracked for global-health, preparedness, implementation, humanitarian and training opportunities.

Philanthropy

Wellcome, Gates, Novo Nordisk Foundation and major challenge networks are included, but invitation-only and open-call routes are deliberately kept distinct.

Life sciences & industry

Industry funding and investigator-sponsored-study portals are treated conservatively. A corporate research page is not automatically presented as an open grant.

Important: this service supports discovery and decision-making; it does not replace the funder’s eligibility rules, guidance notes or application portal.

SOURCE STATES

Know what “verified” means

Every source carries a visible state badge so you can distinguish structured records from link-only discovery.

structured_beta

Structured beta

Source-specific extraction is available. Eligibility still requires source verification.

structured_beta_detail

Detail-page beta

Verified detail pages can be structured, but the index may not reflect all currently open calls.

partial

Partial

Authoritative source tracked, but call-level normalization is incomplete.

index_only

Index only

Official funding index is linked, but no structured call is asserted.

manual_verify

Manual verify

Portal or corporate page requires human verification before it is treated as an open opportunity.

TRUST MODEL

Grounded like a research workflow

The interface inherits PhiriLab’s provenance-first agentic design: source text is data, not instruction.

1

Primary-source ingest

Funding pages, portals and APIs are retrieved from authoritative funder endpoints.

2

Deterministic normalization

Dates, amounts and source metadata are parsed with explicit warnings and no hidden eligibility inference.

3

Evidence-first presentation

The public interface shows provenance, source state and uncertainty rather than a black-box yes/no.

4

Human verification

Users confirm final eligibility, deadlines and submission rules at the funder’s primary source.

ABOUT THE CREATOR

Prof. Peter Phiri, PhD

Clinical academic, cognitive behavioural psychotherapist, global mental health researcher and founder of PhiriLab.

Peter’s work spans culturally adapted CBT, psychosis, digital and VR-assisted interventions, research governance, real-world mental-health data and international research capacity. He chairs the Pan-African CBT Network and is a Visiting Academic at the University of Southampton.

Global Funding Intelligence grew from a practical problem: excellent researchers and community innovators—particularly across Global Majority settings—often spend too much time searching fragmented funding sites, interpreting opaque eligibility rules and discovering opportunities too late. This project aims to reduce that friction while preserving the funder as the final authority.

Independence safeguard

Financial, technical or institutional support does not confer influence over opportunity verification, eligibility assessment, source-state decisions or ranking.

  • Verification remains evidence-led.
  • Funders remain the final authority for their calls.
  • Sponsored relationships should be disclosed transparently.
  • Unknown eligibility remains unknown rather than being inferred.
