TRUST MODEL

Grounded like a research workflow

The interface inherits PhiriLab’s provenance-first agentic design: source text is data, not instruction.

1

Primary-source ingest

Funding pages, portals and APIs are retrieved from authoritative funder endpoints.

2

Deterministic normalization

Dates, amounts and source metadata are parsed with explicit warnings and no hidden eligibility inference.

3

Evidence-first presentation

The public interface shows provenance, source state and uncertainty rather than a black-box “yes/no”.

4

Human verification

Users always confirm final eligibility and submission rules at the funder’s primary source.
